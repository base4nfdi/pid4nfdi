The UC4B2026 conference will bring together the Base4NFDI project, its funded basic services, and the National Research Data Infrastructure (NFDI) community. The two-day event will focus on exchanging ideas, collaborating, and having hands-on discussions about shared research data management (RDM) services. The conference will take place in Berlin from November 24 to 25, 2026. 
The PID4NFDI team will be present with a poster on the basic service outputs, and we look forward to connecting with you.
Further details can be found on the [conference website](https://base4nfdi.de/news-events/events/user-conference-2026).
